The Situation at Juan's Pet Paradise

It's end of March 2024. Juan opens QBO and calls you confused: "My QBO balance and my bank statement don't match. Can you figure out what's wrong?"

You open QBO and pull up the March bank statement. The bank shows a closing balance of $34,390.00. But QBO is showing something different. Your job: find every transaction on the bank statement, match it to QBO, find the 3 discrepancies, fix them, and get the difference to exactly $0.00.

When the difference hits zero — Juan's books are accurate. The CPA can trust the numbers. The tax return is based on solid data. That's the value of bank reconciliation.

📌
What you'll master in S01: Setting up the reconciliation in QBO · Matching transactions line by line · Identifying timing differences vs recording errors · Fixing discrepancies · Generating the Reconciliation Report · Locking the period.
💡
Phase 1 = Foundation: S01 covers one bank account with clean transactions and 3 clear discrepancies. The Reconciliation Specialization Module goes deeper — 3 accounts simultaneously (checking + 2 credit cards), multi-platform reconciliation (Shopify + PayPal + Stripe), 40+ transactions, and a full client reconciliation package. 🔥
1

Read the Bank Statement

Open the Bank Statement tab. Note three things: Opening balance · Closing balance · Statement date. These anchor the whole reconciliation.

2

Open Reconciliation in QBO

Accounting → Reconcile → Select Checking → Enter statement date 03/31/2024 → Enter ending balance $34,390.00 → Start Reconciling.

3

Match transactions line by line

Work through the QBO transaction list. For each item on the bank statement — find it in QBO and check it off. Same date + same amount + same payee = check.

4

Find the 3 discrepancies

Open the Find the 3 Errors tab. Three items won't match. Identify each one — is it a timing difference, recording error, or missing entry?

5

Fix and hit $0.00

Fix the two errors. Leave the timing item unchecked. Watch the difference field hit $0.00. Click Finish Now → Generate the Reconciliation Report.

Coach Mic — Finding the 3 Differences · Think out loud — what does each discrepancy mean?
🔍
3 discrepancies. 3 different types. 3 different fixes. Read each one carefully before touching QBO. Understand WHAT it is and WHY before you fix it.
⚠️

Discrepancy 1 — Wrong Amount (Recording Error)

T-04 · VetMed Supplies · Mar 10 · Fix this → update QBO

❌ What QBO Shows

VetMed Supplies payment recorded as $420.00

✅ What Bank Shows

VetMed Supplies payment cleared for $240.00

🔧
What happened: Digits were transposed — $420 entered instead of $240. Fix: Expenses → find VetMed Mar 10 → open → change amount from $420 to $240 → add memo "Corrected — transposed digits" → Save → return to reconciliation → check it off. Impact: Expenses decrease $180 → Net Income increases $180.
❌

Discrepancy 2 — Missing Entry (Bank Fee $35)

T-12 · Bank Maintenance Fee · Mar 31 · Add to QBO

❌ What QBO Shows

Nothing — bank fee was never recorded

✅ What Bank Shows

Monthly maintenance fee $35.00 deducted Mar 31

🔧
Fix: +New → Expense → Date: 03/31/2024 → Payee: First National Bank → Category: Bank Charges & Fees → Amount: $35.00 → Payment account: Checking → Save → return to reconciliation → check it off. Journal entry: DR Bank Charges $35 / CR Checking $35.
❌

Discrepancy 3 — Missing Entry (Interest Income $45)

T-13 · Interest Earned · Mar 31 · Add to QBO

❌ What QBO Shows

Nothing — interest income was never recorded

✅ What Bank Shows

Interest earned $45.00 credited Mar 31

🔧
Fix: +New → Bank Deposit → Date: 03/31/2024 → Account: Interest Income → Amount: $45.00 → Deposit to: Checking → Save → return to reconciliation → check it off. Journal entry: DR Checking $45 / CR Interest Income $45.
⏳

Timing Item — Outstanding Check (Leave Unchecked)

T-14 · Check #5503 GroomPro Tools · $380 · DO NOT fix

⏳ In QBO

Check #5503 written Mar 31 → recorded in QBO → $380.00

📅 Not on Bank Yet

Check hasn't cleared the bank yet → will appear on April statement

✅
DO NOT fix this. This is a timing difference — completely normal. The check was written and recorded in QBO on Mar 31, but the bank processes it in April. Leave it unchecked in the reconciliation. QBO accounts for it automatically as an "Outstanding Check." It will clear next month.
