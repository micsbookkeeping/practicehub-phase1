Throughout all 6 labs, you'll work with one practice client — Juan's Pet Paradise in San Antonio, Texas.

Juan runs a vet clinic, a grooming service, and a pet retail shop. He sells in-store and on Shopify. He has 3 W-2 employees and 2 contractors. And his books — let's just say — need a lot of attention. 😄

PART 6 The 6 Labs — What You'll Practice 3 min 10:00 – 13:00
🖥️
Screen share: Briefly open each lab and show the hero section — just 5–10 seconds each. Don't go into the content — just show it exists and looks professional.
Say
S01 — Bank Reconciliation. This is the #1 skill US clients test you on. Before they hire you, before they trust you — they will ask: "Can you reconcile my accounts?" After S01, your answer is yes. You'll work with Juan's real March 2024 bank statement, find three deliberate discrepancies, fix them, hit $0.00, and generate the reconciliation report.

S02 — AR/AP Mastery. Money coming in, money going out. 18 transactions — invoices, customer payments, vendor bills, three missing entries, aging reports. Juan calls you frustrated because his vendors are threatening to stop delivery. You fix it.

S03 — Inventory Basics. FIFO costing. Shopify plus in-store. What happens to COGS when one inventory layer runs out and you move to the next. Real stock adjustment for missing product.

S04 — US Payroll Simulator. The one everyone fears. W-2 employees. 1099 contractors. FICA, FUTA, Form 941. By the end of S04 — you can explain FICA to any US client in 30 seconds.

S05 — Cleanup Specialist. Someone else made the mess. You get paid to fix it. 14 real errors — personal expenses mixed with business, wrong categories, duplicates, missing entries. Before and after P&L showing $5,805 difference. This is one of the highest-paid bookkeeping services.

S06 — Client Crisis Mode. No numbers in this one — all judgment. IRS notice. Angry client at 10pm. Someone asks you to falsify a record. Scope creep. You find your own error. Five real scenarios. You'll know exactly what to say — and what never to say.
