📋 S01 Guided Solution — Coach Mic's Walkthrough
The 3 discrepancies explained · What each fix does to the reports
1
Discrepancy 1 — Wrong Amount: VetMed Supplies
QBO showed $420.00 but the bank showed $240.00. Digits were transposed when the payment was entered. Fix: edit the QBO expense entry from $420 to $240. Impact: Expenses decrease by $180 → Net Income increases by $180 → Balance Sheet equity improves.
2
Discrepancy 2 — Missing Entry: Bank Fee $35
The bank deducted a $35 monthly maintenance fee automatically — but nobody recorded it in QBO. Fix: + New → Expense → Payee: First National Bank → Category: Bank Charges & Fees → Amount: $35 → Date: 03/31/2024. Journal entry: DR Bank Charges $35 / CR Checking $35.
3
Discrepancy 3 — Missing Entry: Interest Income $45
The bank credited $45 interest earned — but it was never recorded in QBO. Fix: + New → Bank Deposit → Account: Interest Income → Amount: $45 → Date: 03/31/2024. Journal entry: DR Checking $45 / CR Interest Income $45.
✓
Timing Item — Check #5503 GroomPro Tools $380 (Leave Unchecked)
This check was written and recorded correctly in QBO on March 31 — but the bank hasn't processed it yet. This is an outstanding check — a timing difference, not an error. Leave it unchecked. It will appear on the April bank statement.
