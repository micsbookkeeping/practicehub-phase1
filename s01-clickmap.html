+$45

−$380

Wrong ending balance
Check the Ending Balance field. It must be $34,390.00 exactly — not $34,200, not $34,300. Click the ending balance field → correct it → difference will recalculate.
Still not $0
Work backwards from the difference. The remaining number tells you exactly what's missing or wrong. A difference of −$215 means VetMed ($180) + bank fee ($35) are both unfixed.
